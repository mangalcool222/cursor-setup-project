Podcast 1: Media as Marketing

Source: [https://openthreads.co/3/transcript]

Insight:
Corey treats marketing as building a media engine — creating content consistently to build an audience that later converts into users.

Key Points:
	•	Content acts as a long-term growth engine
	•	Consistent creation builds trust and visibility
	•	Community feedback helps improve the product
	•	Founders should lead content efforts early
	•	Build audience first, then monetize
	•	Owned audience reduces dependency on platforms
