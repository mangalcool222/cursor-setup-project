Video 2: Audience Growth Hacks

Source: https://www.gregisenberg.com/

Insight:
Greg’s strategy focuses on building distribution through multiple platforms and then converting that audience into owned channels, creating long-term growth moats.

Key Points:
	•	Use platform arbitrage (move early to emerging platforms)
	•	Convert audience into owned channels like newsletters or communities
	•	Build a weekly content system to keep audience engaged
	•	Superfans act as organic growth drivers
	•	Focus on retention and shareability over reach
	•	Dominate a niche before expanding
