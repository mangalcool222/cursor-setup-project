Video 2: Solopreneur Business Model (Low-Ticket → High Trust)

Insight:
This content explains how a one-person business can be built using consistent niche content, low-ticket products, and trust-based monetization.
In the context of Reddit, it highlights building credibility first, validating demand, and monetizing gradually without aggressive selling.

Key Points:
	•	Build a non-scalable by design system: focus on simple content + minimal operations (no team, no meetings)
	•	Use a content funnel: social (attention) → newsletter (trust) → product (conversion)
	•	Start with low-ticket offers (~$50) to build trust and initial buyers
	•	Gradually upsell to higher-value or recurring products (subscriptions/templates)
	•	Prioritize profit and lifestyle over scaling aggressively
	•	Stay deeply niche-focused instead of being broad or “multifaceted”
	•	Even non-experts can monetize — knowledge itself is valuable
	•	Expand platforms only after mastering one (LinkedIn → others)
	•	Use audience validation before building products (surveys, feedback)
	•	AI can help with ideation, but real value comes from tested experience
