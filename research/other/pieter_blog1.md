Blog 1: Real Product Validation

Source: [https://levels.io/idea-validation/]

Insight:
Pieter emphasizes that real validation comes from actual payments, not user opinions. For Reddit marketing, this means testing demand through actions (like pre-orders) instead of relying only on feedback.

Key Points:
	•	Talking to users is not enough for validation
	•	Pre-sell landing pages test real demand
	•	No sales despite traffic = strong negative signal
	•	Financial pressure forces better decision-making
	•	Focus on testing willingness to pay, not just interest
	•	Build fast and validate through real transactions
