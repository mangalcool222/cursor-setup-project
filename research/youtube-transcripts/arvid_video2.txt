Video 2: AI Liability Landmines

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKZOtRodPjU

Insight:
This content shows the risks of blindly relying on AI tools. 
For Reddit-based growth, it suggests that authenticity and human judgment are more reliable 
than over-automating interactions or engagement.

Key Points:
- AI agents can misinterpret or damage user data, so safeguards like rate limits and soft deletes are important  
- External bots or agents can overload systems, requiring defensive infrastructure similar to security protection  
- Internal mistakes (like connecting to production systems) highlight the need for sandboxing and backups  
- Businesses are responsible for AI outcomes, not tool providers  
- Long-term advantage comes from unique human data and relationships, not AI tools alone
