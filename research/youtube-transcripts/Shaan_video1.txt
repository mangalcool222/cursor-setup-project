Episode: Audience Growth Session  
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KFvHnWofNdY  

Insight:  
Shaan explains how to build a loyal audience and turn that attention into a customer channel, emphasizing distribution-first thinking over product-first approaches.  

Key Points:  
- Focus on building loyalty, not just reach  
- Audience can become a direct acquisition channel  
- Content should be designed for engagement and retention  
- Distribution is a core business asset, not just marketing
