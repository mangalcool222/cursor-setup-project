Video: Startup Without Funding

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6reLWfFNer0]

Insight:
Pieter’s bootstrap strategy focuses on launching products in communities like Reddit, Product Hunt, and Hacker News to validate ideas and acquire early users without paid marketing.

Key Points:
	•	Build multiple projects to increase chances of success
	•	Launch on multiple platforms simultaneously (PH + HN + Reddit + Twitter)
	•	Use feedback forms to collect emails during early stages
	•	Ship MVP quickly and iterate based on user feedback
	•	Leverage communities for organic reach and validation
	•	Avoid reliance on funding; focus on execution and speed
