Blog: Faceless YouTube Formula

Source: https://www.gregisenberg.com/blog/faceless-youtube-formula
    
Insight:
Greg explains how creators can use content arbitrage to grow fast — by identifying underutilized platforms and building direct audience ownership.

Key Points:
	•	Content arbitrage: Identify platforms with low competition and high reach
	•	Build direct relationships via newsletters
	•	Empower superusers to scale communities
	•	Prioritize retention over acquisition
	•	Create consistent weekly value to maintain engagement
	•	Owned audience is more valuable than platform followers
