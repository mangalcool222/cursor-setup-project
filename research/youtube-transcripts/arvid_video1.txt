Video 1: Building in Public Risks

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PWF_1_r0Dl8

Insight:
This content highlights why founders must be careful about what they share publicly. 
In the context of Reddit, this means sharing insights and experiences while avoiding 
specific product details that can be easily replicated by others.

Key Points:
- The old playbook of openly sharing metrics (like MRR) worked earlier, but now enables fast cloning using AI tools  
- Founders should share operational learnings that are valuable but difficult to replicate  
- A good filter for content: “interesting to follow” but “hard to clone”  
- Customer relationships and execution still remain strong defensible advantages  
- In today’s AI environment, even early-stage ideas can attract competition if overshared
