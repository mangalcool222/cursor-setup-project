## Part 2: Research Project

### Topic Chosen
Reddit Marketing for B2B SaaS (with a focus on Cold Start Engine)

### Why I Chose This
Instead of choosing saturated channels like cold outreach, I focused on Reddit as an underutilized but high-intent acquisition channel for early-stage B2B SaaS.

### What I Am Building
A structured research base of high-signal operators and their content to later derive a practical Reddit GTM playbook.

The goal is not just to collect content, but to identify repeatable patterns that can be applied to real SaaS growth.

### How I Selected Experts
- Real operators (founders / builders)  
- Evidence of actual experimentation  
- Recent and consistent content  
- Focus on community-first growth  

### Repository Structure
```
/research
   ├── sources.md
   ├── linkedin-posts/
   ├── youtube-transcripts/
   └── other/
```

### Early Observations
- Reddit rewards authenticity, not promotion  
- High-performing posts are experience-driven, not advice-driven  
- Founders who succeed spend time engaging before posting  
- Comments matter more than posts for visibility  

### Next Step
Using this dataset, I will build a Reddit GTM playbook for early-stage B2B SaaS.
