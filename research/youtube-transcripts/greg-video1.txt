Video 1: Community Building Framework

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e9J9s2_DAKE&t=3s

Insight:
Greg Isenberg’s framework focuses on building communities in phases — Engagement → Retention → Monetization. This aligns perfectly with Reddit-style growth where value and interaction come before promotion.

Key Points:
	•	Identify early superfans and give them ownership roles (admins/mods)
	•	Start with niche content to build a highly engaged audience
	•	Move users into owned communities (Discord/Slack)
	•	Track “invite friends” as a stronger growth signal than follower count
	•	Focus on organic growth before spending on ads
	•	Achieve community-market fit before monetization
