Video 1: SaaS Growth Secrets

Source: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h_7qiQHp1lo]

Insight:
Corey Haines emphasizes combining customer research with build-in-public strategies. Sharing real metrics and learnings helps attract users and validate ideas through community engagement.

Key Points:
	•	Customer interviews guide feature prioritization
	•	Community feedback validates product decisions
	•	Sharing real metrics (churn, LTV, activation) builds trust
	•	Use structured frameworks to understand user psychology
	•	Focus on high-impact work instead of low-value tasks
	•	Align team around customer feedback for faster iteration
