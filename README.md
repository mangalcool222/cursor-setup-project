## Part 2: Research Project

### Topic Chosen
**Reddit Marketing for B2B SaaS (with a focus on Cold Start Engine)**

### Why I Chose This
Instead of choosing saturated channels like cold outreach, I focused on Reddit as an underutilized but high-intent acquisition channel for early-stage B2B SaaS.

---

### What I Am Building
I am building a structured research repository of 10 high-signal operators and their real content (YouTube transcripts, LinkedIn posts, blogs) to reverse-engineer how Reddit can be used as a cold-start acquisition engine.

The goal is to move from scattered content → extracted insights → repeatable Reddit growth patterns that can be applied to early-stage B2B SaaS.

---

### Data Collection Approach
- YouTube transcripts were collected using manual extraction and AI-assisted parsing (Claude Code / Codex)  
- LinkedIn posts were manually collected and structured into insight + key points format  
- Blogs and external sources were added under the `/other` folder  

Instead of dumping raw data, I converted each piece into structured insights to make pattern extraction easier in later stages.

---

### How I Selected Experts
- Real operators (founders / builders)  
- Evidence of actual experimentation  
- Recent and consistent content  
- Focus on community-first growth  

---

### Repository Structure
```
/research
   ├── sources.md
   ├── linkedin-posts/
   ├── youtube-transcripts/
   └── other/
```

---

### Early Observations
- Reddit punishes promotion but rewards lived experience  
- Posts that perform well are narrative-driven (“what happened”) not instructional (“what to do”)  
- Comment sections act as distribution multipliers  
- Validation and feedback loops happen faster than traditional channels  
- Communities can act as both acquisition + research engines simultaneously  

---

### Next Step
Using this dataset, I will synthesize insights across all experts to build a “Reddit Cold Start Engine” playbook.

---

### Note
This project focuses on depth over volume — 10 high-quality sources were chosen intentionally to extract meaningful and repeatable patterns rather than surface-level insights.
